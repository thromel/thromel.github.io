# Phase 12 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the Dual-Audience Complete Overhaul. Generated by `gsd-ui-researcher`; the checker upgrades this draft after all six dimensions pass.

---

## Contract Basis

`12-CONTEXT.md` supplies the locked decisions; `12-RESEARCH.md` supplies audience, accessibility, performance, imagery, and motion findings. The current canonical UI supplies the progressive shell baseline. The prior dossier review is evidence only: do not transplant its stale branch, full-homepage archive, faux thumbnails, card density, or reveal effects.

## Design Intent

The visual concept is **Alberta field notebook × systems evidence ledger**: warm editorial paper, mineral ink, spruce actions, restrained copper signals, purposeful artifact imagery, fine rules, numbered evidence records, and visible verification dates.

The site must feel authored, scholarly, and technically rigorous. It must not resemble a startup dashboard, a generic portfolio template, or a decorated CV.

### Non-negotiable boundaries

- One site serves professors and engineering recruiters. Never add an audience toggle, filtered mode, duplicated page tree, or audience-specific hidden content.
- `Research` and `Engineering` remain first-class, visible paths in the header, hero, and footer.
- The public introduction starts with the University of Alberta trajectory, then bridges to about three years of professional software-engineering experience and former IQVIA work.
- Render the exact research-interest family: `AI4SE · LLM4Coding · Trustworthy AI · long-horizon coding agents · AI for SRE`.
- Do not use “open-source program analysis” as positioning copy. Name the concrete evidence instead, such as “RefactoringMiner contributor” or the exact tool/repository.
- GTA, GRA, and GRAF may appear only inside dated work-experience records. They must not appear in the hero, identity markers, research agenda, section intros, or footer.
- Every maintained route uses the canonical CSS/JS/shared-shell layer. Do not add page-local visual systems, a second theme model, or a framework runtime.
- Core content exists in semantic HTML before JavaScript runs. JavaScript enhances theme, navigation, and the compact GitHub count only.
- Do not visually equate peer-reviewed work with submissions, work in progress, abstract-only summaries, research systems, or archived builds.
- Do not use generic equal-weight card grids, glassmorphism, purple/neon gradients, particle fields, parallax, carousels, skill bars, contribution heatmaps as primary proof, giant technology clouds, or decorative scroll-reveal motion.

## Visual Grammar

- The recurring motif is an evidence trace: a two-digit section index, a fine horizontal rule, a short uppercase mono label, a human-readable heading, and a verified/status line.
- On viewports at least 960px wide, major sections reserve a 64px index gutter. On smaller viewports the index moves above the heading.
- Copper appears as a short register mark, focus ring, or evidence-boundary rule—not as a filled panel or decorative wash.
- Spruce identifies actionable links and the single primary action in a region.
- Essential text always sits on a solid surface. No gradient, photograph, or texture may be its contrast surface.
- The only decorative field-note treatment is a static 1px notebook margin rule beside the homepage identity copy and major evidence groups. It disappears below 720px. Do not add global ruled-paper or noise overlays.
- Prefer open ledger rows and split editorial compositions. A bordered container is allowed only when it communicates a distinct state, boundary, or embedded artifact.

## Audience and Information Architecture

### Persistent horizontal header

Desktop order is fixed:

1. Name/wordmark linking to `/`.
2. `Research` linking to `/research/`.
3. `Engineering` linking to `/projects/` (the existing stable route becomes the engineering-work landing page).
4. `Publications` linking to `/publications/`.
5. `Experience` linking to `/experience/`.
6. `CV` as the visually distinct header action linking to `/cv/`.
7. A labelled theme control.

The header is 64px tall, sticky, opaque, and separated by a 1px rule. It must never use blur, transparency, or glass effects. Home remains reachable through the wordmark. Secondary routes—About, Education, Contributions, Achievements, News, Learning, Posts, and profile destinations—remain visible in the footer and contextual route links rather than expanding the desktop header into a sitemap.

At widths below 720px, the wordmark, `Menu` button, and theme button remain in the first row. The menu opens an in-flow panel below the header; it is not a modal drawer or side rail. With JavaScript disabled, the navigation is visible and usable.

### Footer directory

Use three compact groups:

- `Explore`: Research, Engineering, Publications, Experience, Contributions.
- `Background`: About, Education, Achievements, News, Learning, Posts, CV.
- `Profiles`: Email, GitHub, Scholar, LinkedIn, ORCID, Source.

Repeat the University of Alberta/current-status statement only if it is generated from the same ISO-backed data as the hero. Do not add a second biography.

## Homepage Contract

The homepage is a website abstract, not a complete dossier. It contains exactly the following seven regions in this order.

### 1. Identity and audience paths

The first viewport must contain, in this order:

- Current Alberta status, sourced from structured currentness data. On 2026-08-02 it renders as `University of Alberta · Incoming M.Sc. in Computing Science`; the state must update when the underlying ISO range changes.
- `Tanzim Hossain Romel` as the only page H1.
- Thesis: `I study how AI agents behave in real software systems—and how to make their decisions inspectable, reliable, and trustworthy.`
- Bridge: `About three years of professional software-engineering experience, formerly at IQVIA.` The linked University of Alberta, IQVIA, and IQVIA product names use authoritative destinations.
- Interests: `AI4SE · LLM4Coding · Trustworthy AI · long-horizon coding agents · AI for SRE`.
- Three paths: `Explore research`, `Review engineering work`, and `Download CV`.

The hero contains no assistantship acronyms, stats strip, technology cloud, institution-chip wall, awards, or secondary social-link cluster. Email and profiles appear later.

Desktop uses a 7/5 split with copy first and a single 3:4 portrait second. Mobile keeps copy and all three paths before the portrait. The portrait is at most 288px wide, has intrinsic dimensions, and is the only eager/high-priority image.

### 2. Selected evidence

Show exactly four selected records: two research and two engineering. Render them as alternating ledger rows, not a 2×2 card grid.

Every record contains:

1. Index and evidence type.
2. Problem or research question.
3. Tanzim’s precise contribution or role.
4. Defensible result or current status.
5. Methods/technology, limited to five terms.
6. Human-readable date plus machine-readable `datetime` or `last_verified` value.
7. One primary proof destination and at most one secondary destination.

At least one research record appears before the first engineering record. At least one engineering record demonstrates shipped or merged evidence rather than a technology list. A meaningful diagram, paper figure, release image, or repository artifact may accompany at most two of the four records; records without a real artifact remain text-only.

### 3. Research agenda

Show exactly three connected question-led rows:

1. Long-horizon coding agents: context, memory, and software evolution.
2. AI for SRE: evaluation and recovery in live reliability conditions.
3. Trustworthy AI: agent choices, security boundaries, and inspectable evidence.

Use AI4SE and LLM4Coding as explicit umbrella labels. Each row links to one current artifact or detail page and carries a truthful status. End with `View the full research agenda` linking to `/research/`.

### 4. Engineering experience

Lead with `About three years in software engineering` and a selected former-IQVIA record. Show at most two role/system records and three outcome bullets per record. Each record states role, dates, scope, outcomes, methods, and direct employer/product/repository evidence.

Do not reproduce the full IQVIA responsibility list, show unverified metrics, or render a skill-card grid. End with `View complete experience` and `Browse engineering projects`.

### 5. Publications and open-source proof

Use two adjacent but semantically separate ledgers:

- Research outputs: at most two items, with citation/status, contribution, date, and direct paper/summary/artifact link.
- Open source: at most three curated contribution records, with repository, exact contribution, merged-PR/release proof, and date.

The live GitHub count is supporting context on `/contributions/`; it must not displace curated proof or become a homepage vanity metric.

### 6. Recent news and education trajectory

Show at most three dated milestones. The Alberta transition is first while it is current. Use `<time datetime="YYYY-MM-DD">` and one-sentence summaries. Link to `/news/` and `/education/` rather than reproducing either archive.

### 7. Collaboration and contact

Use the heading `Discuss reliable agent systems` and one sentence inviting research or engineering conversation. Primary action: `Email Tanzim`. Secondary links: GitHub, Scholar, LinkedIn, ORCID, and CV. Do not add a contact form.

## Typography

Use exactly four size roles and two weights across the redesigned system.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height | Font |
|------|------|--------|-------------|------|
| Label / metadata | 14px | 600 | 1.4 | Local monospace stack |
| Body / navigation | 16px | 400 | 1.6 | Local humanist sans stack |
| Heading / thesis | 24px | 600 for headings, 400 for thesis | 1.2 | Local editorial serif stack |
| Display / page H1 | `clamp(40px, 5vw, 56px)` | 600 | 1.05 | Local editorial serif stack |

- The only weights are regular 400 and semibold 600. Remove 500 and 700 from the loaded font contract.
- No user-facing text is smaller than 14px.
- Mono never carries paragraphs, navigation, or CTA labels.
- Uppercase is limited to short evidence labels of at most four words, with 0.08em tracking.
- Display headings use a maximum 18-character measure; section headings use 28 characters; prose uses the shared 62–68-character reading measure.
- Link underlines remain visible in body copy. Do not distinguish links through color alone.

## Evidence and Status Vocabulary

Use only status text supported by structured data. Preferred labels are:

- `Peer-reviewed publication`
- `Under review at {venue}`
- `Submitted to {venue}`
- `Active research`
- `Emerging research direction`
- `Research system`
- `Open-source tool`
- `Merged contribution`
- `Abstract-level summary · manuscript not public`
- `Archived build`

Render status beside a verified or activity date. Never shorten `submitted`, `under review`, or `abstract-only` to `published`, and never infer adoption, deployment, funding, affiliation, or metrics from a repository or draft.

## Interaction Contract

### Links and actions

- Body/evidence links are underlined in their default state. Header links use an active underline; buttons use border/fill plus text.
- Hover may thicken an underline or shift color only. Do not hide information until hover, lift cards, scale media, or move controls.
- Primary and secondary actions are visually distinct but both retain a 44px minimum target.
- External evidence may open a new tab only for PDFs, repositories, papers, and institutional destinations. Every `_blank` link includes `rel="noopener noreferrer"` and an accessible “opens in new tab” indication.
- Avoid `Learn more`, `View more`, `Click here`, and bare URL labels. Use the exact destination or action.

### Mobile menu

- A real `button` controls the in-flow navigation with `aria-controls` and `aria-expanded`.
- `Menu` becomes `Close` while expanded.
- Escape closes it; selecting a route closes it; focus returns to the disclosure button when Escape closes it.
- No focus trap is used because the disclosure is non-modal.
- With JavaScript unavailable, the navigation remains expanded rather than disappearing.

### Theme

- First visit follows `prefers-color-scheme`; a stored `light` or `dark` override wins on later visits.
- The small first-paint script sets `data-theme` before CSS paints. A storage exception must fall back safely.
- The visible button label/accessible name is `Use dark theme` or `Use light theme`; an unexplained sun/moon icon is insufficient.
- Keep `color-scheme` aligned with the selected theme and update `aria-pressed`.

### Keyboard and focus

- Preserve the skip link and logical DOM/heading order.
- Every interactive element receives a two-tone focus treatment: 2px current surface separation plus a 3px copper outer ring. Focus must remain visible in both themes and may not be clipped by overflow containers or the sticky header.
- `aria-current="page"` is set on the active primary route. Section anchors receive visible focus when targeted through the skip/audience paths.

### Motion

- Transitions are limited to color, underline thickness, and disclosure appearance, 120–160ms maximum.
- No content starts at `opacity: 0`, and no `IntersectionObserver` is required to reveal content.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, smooth scrolling and nonessential transitions are disabled.

## Async and No-JavaScript States

Curated contribution evidence renders statically. The GitHub count makes one compact request, times out after five seconds, and never controls whether proof is visible.

| State | Visible copy / behavior |
|-------|-------------------------|
| Loading | `Checking the current merged contribution count…` with `—` value; polite live region |
| Success | `{count} merged pull requests to external repositories.` plus last-reviewed note |
| Empty | Heading: `No live contribution count returned` Body: `Selected contribution records remain available above. View the evidence or retry the count.` |
| Rate limit | `GitHub is rate-limited right now. The selected evidence remains available; retry the count later.` |
| Timeout | `GitHub took too long to respond. The selected evidence remains available; retry the count.` |
| Error | `GitHub could not refresh the merged contribution count. Review the selected evidence or try again later.` |
| No JavaScript | `Live count unavailable without JavaScript. Selected contribution evidence is shown above.`; do not show a nonfunctional retry button |

The retry label is `Retry count`. Do not use animated skeletons or spinners that imply the curated page is blocked.

## Imagery Contract

- Use only purposeful images: the portrait, a method/architecture figure, a paper cover, a system screenshot, or a linked institution/employer mark.
- Do not manufacture generic icon thumbnails for records that lack a real visual. A text-only ledger row is the correct fallback.
- The portrait is AVIF/WebP, at most 120KB, 3:4, intrinsically sized, `fetchpriority="high"`, and not lazy-loaded.
- Every raster asset is at most 500KB; below-fold assets use `loading="lazy"`, `decoding="async"`, and intrinsic width/height.
- Artifact images use `object-fit: contain` and keep readable captions/source context. Cropping diagrams to `cover` is prohibited.
- Logos are at most 48px in the normal flow and retain their native aspect ratio.
- Informative images have specific alt text. Decorative marks use empty alt text; do not repeat adjacent headings in alt text.

## Accessibility Contract

- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 normal-text contrast, 3:1 large-text contrast, visible non-obscured focus, keyboard operability, and lossless reflow at 320 CSS pixels and 200% zoom.
- Practical targets are at least 44×44px even though WCAG’s minimum is smaller.
- Use one H1, ordered headings, landmarks, lists for record collections, `<time datetime>`, `<dl>` for labelled metadata, and real buttons for actions.
- Status and audience meaning never depend on color, position, image, or icon alone.
- At 320px there is no page-level horizontal scroll. Only code blocks and genuinely wide data tables may have a labelled internal scroll region.
- Theme, navigation, portrait, key evidence links, async messaging, and footer routes are fully usable with keyboard and screen-reader semantics.
- Automated Axe checks must have zero serious or critical violations, but automation does not replace keyboard, zoom, contrast, and no-JavaScript checks.
